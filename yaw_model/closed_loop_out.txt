     The hull enters the push already drifting at 0.15 m/s (the Manual phase's and the brake's tail FORCE;
     the rest wait stops only the yaw) and the push's own tail force adds to it. In a turn the drift
     rotates through the body at the yaw rate, so u v, and with it the Munk moment, goes through one
     cycle per 180 deg of heading (22-27 s at 8-6.7 deg/s). The rate follows: the Munk moment reaches
     twice the 0.076 N m it takes to hold 6.67 deg/s. Only one term does this: without it (no Munk) the
     model tracks like the 1dof plant; without the fins it still fails. The chain7 Stabilized push shows the
     same cycle: 7.59 at 132.2 s, 6.39 at 147.0 s, 7.30 again by 155.9 s. The drift never shows in the
     gate's columns; the gate logs no velocity.

     The 1dof plant fixes the fin share at one. In the runs both vertical fins act from the Manual phase
     on, because the hull drifts forward (u > 0) and LiftDrag.cc:312 gives a fin nothing only when its
     flow comes from behind. Two sources of that drift, both in the model: the pods' net surge while one
     of them swings (from rest the port pod thrusts at once while the starboard pod turns through 90 deg,
     vectored_actuation.py regime A: u 0.19 m/s by the end of the Manual step), and, with that switched
     off ("no surge"), the fins themselves: an open fin's side force makes sway, sway times yaw rate is
     surge through the Coriolis terms, and the forward drift then opens the other fin (later, in the Acro
     push). The surge drag (xUabsU 0.2748) hardly decays it. 1dof with two fins ("1dof f2") gets the
     pushes and releases but not the I-on wind case (0.14 against 0.20-0.22 deg/s): there the flow at the
     fins is the drift's, and the fin damping is the attached-flow K_d u r, not a broadside r|r|.

4. What the model still misses, and why (no verdict disagrees; no number is off by more than the gate's
   own tolerance for it; reproduction_out.txt flags the numbers further off than the two SITL runs are
   from each other)
  gz_cloudship_am_fin_drag                     first wind check -19.47 deg/s (records: chain7 -19.87, chain9 -20.01); vertical fins open at +1..4.7 s: [1, 2, 2, 2, 2]; u +0.0058 m/s at +4.7 s
  gz_airship_vectored_independent_fin          first wind check -1.99 deg/s (records: chain9 -1.97); vertical fins open at +1..4.7 s: [1, 1, 1, 1, 2]; u +0.0000 m/s at +4.7 s
  a) The first wind check on a finned plant is the one phase that starts from exact rest. There the
     fin plugin's gate (flow from behind -> nothing, LiftDrag.cc:312) is decided by sub-mm/s surge
     drift and the roll angle. On the complete Cloudship the model opens the second fin by +2 s, which
     gz does not; the two gz runs disagree with each other too (-19.87, -20.01). Held here: heave and pitch
     (w = 0, theta = 0); the Cloudship's buoyancy sits 0.022 m aft of its centre of mass
     (gz_model.centre_of_volume vs centre_of_mass), a ~2.5 deg nose-down trim the model does not
     have; whether that is what decides the at-rest drift is untested. From the Manual phase on, the
     actuators' own drift (0.15-0.33 m/s) swamps it.
  b) gz_cloudship Manual end / brake start (0.5-0.7 deg/s): those rows sample a 22 deg/s^2 ramp, so
     20-30 ms of harness timing moves them that far (section 3: stick delay 0 vs 50 ms moves them 1.1
     deg/s); chain7 and chain9 differ by 0.5-0.6 on the same two numbers.
  c) The rigid vectored ripple (section 2c) is modelled, but its phase over 100+ s is not a stable
     quantity: the Acro push row rms stays at 0.21 deg/s while the Stabilized push is 0.04.
